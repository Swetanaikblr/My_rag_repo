02 · Choose a chunking strategy

Preparation

Chunking splits long content into smaller passages. Chunk size and overlap affect what the retriever can find.

260
30
Why this matters: tiny chunks can lose context; huge chunks can bring irrelevant text and use more of the LLM's context window. Overlap can preserve information at boundaries.
